# MV5-AM selection-resistant four-panel synthesis gate specification v1

Date frozen: 2026-08-12

Accepted base: `d5c4b7e`

New calculation, ranking, label access, outcome, or clustering state: zero

## Purpose

MV5-AM binds the complete accepted PC20, cosine-chord, nested-192, and
nested-256 retrieval-robustness panels before synthesizing them or choosing a
next action. It is a reporting and continuation gate, not a fifth robustness
experiment. No representation, homology dimension, endpoint, tissue, seed,
estimate, interval, raw p-value, adjusted p-value, rank, or favorable subgroup
may determine what is retained or what happens next.

## Complete evidence requirement

Each panel must contribute all 24 macro estimands, all 24 paired blocked
intervals, and all four prespecified primary contrasts, together with its full
production, prediction-lock, source, validation, repeat, resume, and audit
identity. The synthesis therefore contains exactly 96 macro-estimand rows, 96
interval rows, and 16 primary-test rows. Null, negative, positive,
near-threshold, and multiplicity-adjusted results remain equally visible.

The panels share fold, seed, representation, method-family, endpoint, sample,
study, and tissue axes. They represent different interventions and must not be
pooled into a single effect estimate. Cross-panel summaries are descriptive
and completeness-preserving.

## Landscape definition

The scientific landscape definition is immutable in this sprint: all finite
intervals; the essential H0 interval excluded; every consecutive active level;
exact squared-L2 integration over linear critical-pair segments; H0 and H1
separate; unweighted Euclidean H0/H1 combination descriptive only; no fixed
uniform grid, universal level cap, post-hoc normalization, or level weighting.

## Selection firewall

The continuation helper accepts only structural registries, completeness
bindings, comparability flags, prerequisite-ordered evidence gaps, and eight
prospectively frozen criteria. It rejects estimate, interval, p-value, tissue,
winner, or rank columns. Numerical result rows are published in synthesis
ledgers but consumed zero times by the decision helper. In particular, the
MV5-AL integrated-H1 raw p-value and interval cannot authorize another
configuration or receive privileged reporting.

## Structural decision

The immutable four-position MV5-V configuration sequence is exhausted. No
fifth configuration is authorized. Directed retrieval pairs still do not
identify the complete within-training matrices required for clustering.

The first unresolved prerequisite is the public landscape contract: the
correct exact engine exists and has powered the accepted analyses, while the
general reference API still records that it is not the scientific default.
Passing all eight criteria may authorize only a separate MV5-AN prospective
prefreeze for reconciling public landscape semantics, API, compatibility,
tests, documentation, and migration policy. MV5-AM does not authorize changing
a default.

## Validation and stop boundary

Independent validation must rehash every source, reconstruct all 96/96/16
result rows directly from accepted files, verify axes and full joins, prove the
decision interface consumed no numerical result, reproduce every public ledger
byte-for-byte in two clean builds, and confirm all prohibited execution
counters remain zero.

This sprint excludes PH, landscape, distance, ranking, or outcome
recalculation; clustering; gene/fusion work; new data; Rust or optimization;
default changes; manuscript claims; PDFs/private/reviewer artifacts; pushing;
and `example_run.r`.
