New robustness calculation or outcome access: prohibited

## Purpose

MV5-AA decides whether the robustness program should advance by exactly one
configuration after complete PC20 reporting. It cannot choose among remaining
configurations. The canonical MV5-V queue order is binding: completed PC20,
then cosine-chord geometry, then nested 192 cells, then nested 256 cells.

## Whole-analysis evidence rule

The gate binds the complete MV5-Z bundle: all 150 prediction/outcome groups,
282,800 ranks, 7,200 endpoint rows, 24 macro estimands and intervals, and four
primary tests. No representation, H0/H1 component, tissue, endpoint, seed,
interval, p-value, or favorable sign may control continuation. The PC20 result
is used only to establish complete reporting and to avoid falsely treating one
sensitivity axis as a universal robustness demonstration.

## Scientific rule

PC20 changes retained coordinate dimension while preserving Euclidean point
geometry. Cosine-chord changes radial-scale dependence while preserving 384
cells and the accepted 30 coordinates. Consequently, PC20 cannot answer the
already named cosine-geometry alternative. Cosine remains identifiable,
one-factor-at-a-time, artifact-ready, and reviewer-relevant regardless of
which PC20 subgroup estimates are positive, negative, or null.

Continuation requires all eight frozen criteria: canonical position, complete
unsliced PC20 evidence, a distinct named alternative, one-factor
identifiability, frozen coordinate readiness, bounded resources, the selection
firewall, and a sequential stop boundary. Failure of any criterion rejects
continuation and cannot substitute a later configuration.

## Stop boundary

MV5-AA does not calculate cosine views, PH, landscapes, energy, rankings,
outcomes, or clustering. Labels remain closed. It does not authorize either
nested-cell configuration, alter methods, select a subgroup, set equivalence
margins, change package defaults, begin gene/fusion/new-data/Rust work, make a
manuscript claim, track private/PDF/reviewer material or `example_run.r`, push,
or modify accepted evidence.
