# MV5-AE selection-resistant nested-cell continuation gate specification v1

Date frozen: 2026-08-11

Accepted base: `0b32d76`

New calculation/outcome state: zero

## Purpose

MV5-AE decides whether to continue the prespecified robustness sequence after
complete PC20 and cosine analyses. It may authorize only the canonical next
remaining configuration; it cannot choose a setting because a representation,
homology dimension, tissue, endpoint, seed, estimate, interval, p-value, or
method ranking appears favorable.

The immutable MV5-V order is:

1. 384 cells / 20 PCs / Euclidean — complete;
2. 384 cells / 30 PCs / cosine chord — complete;
3. nested 192 cells / 30 PCs / Euclidean — next eligible;
4. nested 256 cells / 30 PCs / Euclidean — closed.

## Complete prior evidence

Both prior analyses are bound without slicing: 150 prediction groups, 150
outcome groups, 282,800 ranking rows, 3,600 query-method outcomes, 7,200 long
endpoint rows, 24 macro estimands, 24 intervals, and four primary tests each.

The PC20 analysis tested coordinate truncation. The cosine analysis tested
radial-scale geometry. Neither tests sensitivity to the number of cells used to
represent each biological sample. Prior signs, intervals, p-values, and
subgroups are recorded for completeness but are not inputs to the decision
helper.

## Distinct one-factor question

Nested 192 changes only the cell-realization depth from the accepted 384-cell
Euclidean/30-coordinate benchmark. Each 192-cell view must be exactly the first
192 cells of the already frozen deterministic 384-cell realization. This
nesting separates cell-count sensitivity from an unrelated redraw.

Coordinate count remains 30, point metric remains Euclidean, fold-fitted
representation and coordinate source remain unchanged, and the same directed
held-out-query/training-reference pairs and four method families are retained.
SCT and inductive-integrated results remain separate.

## Mandatory decision criteria

Authorization requires all nine criteria:

- canonical position three after positions one and two are complete;
- both complete prior panels bound without result slicing;
- a scientifically distinct unresolved cell-subsampling question;
- one-factor-at-a-time identifiability;
- exact nested source/inclusion readiness for all 150 groups;
- bounded resources supported by six real nested-192 admissions and two
  complete 150-group precedents;
- a decision interface that consumes no subgroup or result statistic;
- a later commitment to the same complete 24-estimand reporting pattern; and
- a stop before rankings, labels, outcomes, clustering, or nested 256.

Failure of any criterion yields no authorization. There is no substitution,
fallback configuration, or outcome-informed repair.

## Authorized label-closed scope

If all criteria pass, exactly 150 nested-192 groups are authorized for a later
calculation: 75 SCT and 75 inductive-integrated groups over 15 folds and five
seeds. The exact scope is 13,500 views, 70,700 biological pairs, 141,400 H0/H1
landscape rows in 720 subchunks, 70,700 matched-energy rows, and 282,800
four-method rows.

H0 and H1 remain separate exact all-active-level persistence-landscape
distances. The raw H0/H1 composite remains descriptive. Energy is recomputed
on the same nested 192-cell coordinates. No universal landscape cap or grid is
introduced.

The 150-row queue authorizes only later label-closed calculation. It does not
authorize ranking, tissue access, outcome evaluation, clustering, within-
training pairs, or nested 256.

## Resources and execution guards

The six real nested-192 admissions completed within 27.052 seconds per group
and below 420 MB RSS. Complete PC20 and cosine calculations required about
3.101 and 2.608 worker-hours. The later conservative envelope is one worker,
600 seconds and 4 GiB process-tree RSS per group, six aggregate worker-hours,
and 4 GiB new private storage.

A later engine must bind the exact source, runtime, queue, implementation, and
subchunk identities before execution. Every group publishes atomically;
partial or stale units are quarantined. Source/axis/nesting/shape/PH/MST/
landscape/energy/method-row failure, cap breach, early ranking/label/outcome
access, or nested-256/clustering access aborts without automatic repair.

Independent validation must reconstruct all source hashes, queue axes, nested
inclusion identities, views, H0 MSTs, exact landscapes, energy values, and all
282,800 method rows without production scientific helpers. Prospective
minimum/maximum groups, clean public assembly, and full immutable resume with
timestamps are required.

## Interpretation and stop boundary

Passing MV5-AE means only that nested-192 calculation is a distinct,
selection-resistant, feasible next sensitivity. It does not predict its
biological result or authorize a robustness, superiority, equivalence, or
default claim.

This sprint executes no nested calculation. It excludes ranking, labels,
outcomes, clustering, within-training pairs, nested 256, gene topology,
cell/gene fusion, new data, Rust optimization, defaults, manuscript claims,
private/PDF/reviewer tracking, pushing, and `example_run.r`.
