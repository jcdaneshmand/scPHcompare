# MV8-A corrected publication figure prefreeze specification v1

Date: 2026-08-16

## Purpose and boundary

MV8-A renders the first seven publication figures prospectively mapped by
MV7-J. It may read validated artifacts and derive display-only bins or layout
coordinates. It may not recompute PH, landscapes, distances, clustering,
metadata outcomes, p-values, ranks, or selections. Figure 8, which must combine
the separate 90-sample blocked integration estimand with other earlier stages,
is deferred to a dedicated cross-stage synthesis so it cannot be visually
confused with the 124-sample descriptive analysis.

Passing MV8-A authorizes author review of the rendered figures. It does not
authorize manuscript submission, a biological claim, new data, or new PH.

## Formats and reproducibility

Each figure is generated by one versioned R script in both editable SVG and
300-dpi PNG. No manuscript or dissertation PDF is copied into the repository.
The renderer writes a machine-readable data/parameter bundle, file hashes,
dimensions, and a source manifest. A clean repeat must reproduce all data
tables exactly and all SVG files byte-for-byte. PNG files must be independently
rerendered with identical pixel dimensions and perceptual content; their binary
identity is also required under the fixed ragg environment.

All text remains live in SVG. Colors use one fixed colorblind-accessible
palette, and every distinction is also encoded by direct labels, shape, line
type, or position. Minimum final-size text is 8 points. Panels use white
backgrounds, dark text, restrained grid lines, and no decorative gradients.

## Figure 1: corrected architecture

Show the complete hierarchy:

`sample -> cell and gene point-cloud views -> separate H0/H1 persistence
diagrams -> all-active-level landscapes -> exact/error-controlled separate
distances -> seed-specific matrices -> label-free PAM plus average-linkage
sensitivity -> descriptive metadata association`.

The figure must label samples as the comparison/clustering units and cells or
genes as within-sample observations. The secondary unweighted composite is a
side branch, never the main path. The figure must explicitly state no fixed
grid, no level cap, and essential H0 excluded.

## Figure 2: cohort and confounding

Show 127 discovered samples partitioned into 124 retained and three excluded
for too few cells. Within the 124, distinguish the 90-sample primary context
from 34 added descriptive samples. Show eight tissues and 18 studies, and show
that all six snRNA-seq samples are nested in substantia nigra/SRA850958. The
figure must state that approach effects are not identifiable and the three
added tissues are single-study.

## Figure 3: diagrams to corrected landscapes

Use one fixed, validated, label-closed cell-view PH artifact selected before
rendering: seed 20260807, sample SRA701877_SRS3279688. Do not display its tissue,
study, or outcome metadata. Show finite H0 and H1 intervals as persistence
diagrams, then illustrate multiple consecutive landscape levels separately for
H0 and H1. The display may evaluate tents on a dense plotting grid, but must
label that grid as visualization only and state that scientific distances are
integrated exactly or with error control across every active level.

The infinite H0 class is excluded. No combined H0/H1 curve is shown as the
primary landscape. H0 and H1 remain separate throughout the primary analysis.

## Figure 4: H1 contribution and H0/composite concordance

Use all 7,626 median-across-seed pair contributions per view. Plot a fixed
log-scaled distribution or empirical cumulative distribution with no pair
selection. Pair it with all 20 H0-versus-composite partition ARIs. Directly
label medians and the one non-identical cell-average seed. State that H1 energy
is small in the secondary composite but H1-only results remain separate.

## Figure 5: label-free stability

Plot all 54 stability rows: six representations by k=2:10, with the frozen
jackknife SE and one-SE threshold. Mark selected k=2 for every representation.
Do not use tissue, study, or approach labels in this figure.

## Figure 6: descriptive alignment

Plot all 120 complete outcome summaries. Separate population, label axis,
algorithm role, and metric. Show seed means with jackknife uncertainty. PAM is
primary and average linkage is sensitivity. No winner, rank, significance
marker, or favorable subset is permitted. The structurally non-estimable
primary-90 approach endpoint must remain explicit outside the heatmap.

## Figure 7: algorithm sensitivity

Plot all 30 PAM-versus-average partition ARIs by representation and seed.
Directly show the high gene-H0 agreement and low H1/cell agreement without
declaring either algorithm superior. Both algorithms use the same label-free
selected k.

## Source and privacy rules

All public panels use public validated summaries where possible. Figure 3 may
read its fixed private PH artifact after checking the accepted MV7-H manifest.
Figure 4 may read the private H1 pair table after checking the MV7-I artifact
manifest, but it may publish only bins/quantiles without sample identifiers.
No private contingency, per-sample cluster membership, confidential reviewer
text, dissertation PDF, or manuscript PDF may enter a figure or public data
bundle.

## Acceptance criteria

- source hashes and fixed figure-3 identity pass;
- seven SVG and seven PNG files render;
- SVGs contain no rasterized text and no legacy first-level/100-grid claim;
- PNGs are at least 2,400 pixels on their long side at 300 dpi;
- all scheduled rows and bins are complete;
- public data bundles contain no sample identifiers except the redacted
  figure-3 source identity in the private provenance record;
- complete figure families are shown without selection;
- landscape language matches MV7-J;
- figure-6 non-estimability and confounding notes are visible;
- repeat hashes and independent structural/image validation pass; and
- figure 8 remains explicitly deferred rather than silently omitted.
