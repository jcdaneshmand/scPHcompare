## MV-06 prerequisite audit

MV-06 requires independent, matched cell and gene components before fusion.
The immutable MV-04 bundle supplies four paired cohort/representation strata:

- `large__sct_whole` and `large__seurat_integration`, 10 samples each;
- `bone__sct_whole` and `bone__integrated`, 4 samples each;
- complete, matching sample axes for cell and gene views;
- separate H0 and H1 matrices with fit-scope-bound median off-diagonal scales.

These objects are eligible for a bounded, label-closed technical fusion pilot.
They are not sufficient for blocked biological evaluation: the 10-sample
multi-tissue panel is a deliberately broad technical pilot, the four-sample
bone panels cannot support the planned blocked endpoint design, and the full
90-sample MV-05 benchmark contains cell-view rather than matched full gene-view
matrices.
