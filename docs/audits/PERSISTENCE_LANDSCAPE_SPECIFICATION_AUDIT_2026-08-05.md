## Artifact forensics

All 124-sample landscape artifacts have shape `5x500`, while all 25-sample bone-marrow artifacts have shape `500x5`. The saved pair `(1,2)` LDM entries match the direct five-level Frobenius/H0-H1 calculation to floating-point tolerance, proving that those LDMs were not first-level-only results.

The finite maximum-death endpoint ratio across samples ranges from 4.21 to 52,410 in seven profiles and reaches 1,305 in bone-marrow SCT Individual. Consequently, the same landscape array column or row can represent radically different filtration values if TDA selected each sample’s grid independently. Index-wise distance between such arrays is not a distance between functions on a common domain.

The correlation between the legacy aggregated curve and the correctly oriented pointwise all-level L2 curve is negative in two 124-sample profiles (`-0.306` integrated; `-0.219` SCT Whole), and only `0.137` for raw. The bone-marrow correlations are high (`0.959–0.999`) because that artifact orientation happened to make `rowMeans()` average levels, but a mean is still not an L2 norm.

The unweighted combined legacy LDMs are generally H0-dominated. Median H1 energy fractions range from approximately `3.04e-7` to `5.81e-2` in the 124-sample analyses and from `1.06e-7` to `3.49e-3` in bone marrow. H1 should therefore be retained and reported, but separately from H0 before any combined summary.

## Analytical fixtures and implementation result

`tests/testthat/test-landscape-specification.R` establishes:

- first-level, mean-level, and L2 level summaries are distinct;
- two translated intervals can yield identical arrays on separate translated grids but a nonzero distance on one common grid;
- an interval `[2,4]` becomes an all-zero landscape on the legacy `[0,1]` grid;
- dimension-specific grids preserve H1 resolution when H0 has a much larger range;
- H0/H1 component and combined distances match hand-calculated values;
- public landscape generation now requests `KK=1` explicitly and canonicalizes orientation, preserving current scientific behavior while removing dependency on a package default.

Focused locked-environment result: 21 expectations passed after the final contract tests, with no failures, warnings, or skips. The complete source-loaded suite had no failures (two real-subprocess tests skipped under CRAN-mode guards), and the built source package completed `R CMD check --no-manual --no-build-vignettes` with `Status: OK`.

## Repairs made versus decisions deferred

Unambiguous repairs implemented:

- explicit `KK=1` in the existing public compatibility path;
- finite-interval filtering and stable time-by-level orientation;
- internal versioned contract registry and validation;
- common-grid derivation, dimension-specific grids, trapezoidal L2, and component-first H0/H1 distance helpers;
- reproducible legacy-artifact profiling and discriminating regression fixtures.

Scientifically consequential changes deliberately deferred:

- activating all-level L2 as the package default;
- choosing comparison strata, grid range, grid resolution, or level cap for the rerun;
- replacing downstream legacy `rowMeans()` paths;
- regenerating LDMs, clusters, statistics, figures, and claims.

These require the author-team decisions listed in the proposed specification and corrected-run evidence.

## Risks and dependencies

- Persistence diagrams themselves remain conditional on P1-01 (observation-unit orientation) and S-006 (filtration/truncation comparability).
- A pointwise H0/H1 curve cannot be formed on the dimension-specific primary grids without a separately declared display grid.
- The existing curve “Wasserstein” calculations require the separate statistical audit, especially for signed or zero-mass curves.
- H1 is scientifically plausible and computationally covered, but this audit does not establish that H1 carries biological signal.
