# MV11-K threshold-free cross-view review prefreeze

This prefreeze was written before inspecting the closed MV11-I agreement
values. The review must retain all 20 H0/H1 × method × K summaries, report the
five-seed mean/median/minimum/maximum without a magnitude threshold, and compute
exactly ten identity-matched K=3 minus K=2 mean-ARI contrasts. The four PAM
rows may be highlighted only because PAM was prospectively primary; outcomes
may not select a method or K.

Adjusted Rand agreement is symmetric. A larger value means the cell- and
gene-side partitions are more similar for that fixed method, seed, dimension,
and K; it does not mean either view is superior. No view ranking, fusion,
label/outcome association, inference, biological interpretation, or manuscript
claim is authorized.
