# ADR-017: Complete integrated coordinates before authorizing integrated PH

## Status

Accepted and executed on 2026-08-09.

## Context

MV5-F established that fixed-D1-panel, label-closed inductive integration was
correct and probably feasible, but only four structural fold groups were run.
The downstream cost projection still depended partly on pilot extrapolation.
Executing PH in the same sprint would have prevented a clean measured gate at
the coordinate boundary.

## Decision

Execute all 75 fold-seed integrated-coordinate groups with the exact MV5-F
contract and one heavy worker. Require immutable references, query-specific
active frozen-panel subsets without replacement, no label transfer, ordered
384-by-30 coordinate matrices, atomic validated resume, independent validation,
a byte-identical complete-group repeat, and a zero-rebuild resume proof.

Stop before PH. Reproject the complete integrated cell-primary route from the
measured coordinate stage plus the accepted D3/D4/D5 workloads with the frozen
25% reserve. Authorize integrated cell PH only as a separate sprint if every
correctness, scope, time, memory, and storage gate passes.

## Evidence

All 75 groups completed across 15 studies and five seeds, producing 6,750
coordinate views and 450 query mappings with zero failures. Every reference was
immutable. Query mappings retained 496 to 500 frozen-panel genes and used 145
to 907 anchors.

Independent validation passed 675/675 checks across nine categories. The
admission and production copies of `mv05g_group__SRA550660__20260805` have the
same exact RDS SHA-256
`eacde0ed761d81a6a347876b1b48fbdb5b0adfb14cef845ac5ae6cdb2926b7d7`.
A completed-queue resume rebuilt zero groups and preserved all 75 hashes.

Measured coordinate production used 9.107 worker-hours, 806.4 MB, 3.077 GiB
peak process-tree RSS, and at most 790.95 seconds per group. The reserved total
through integrated retrieval projects to 12.379 worker-hours and 1.335 GB,
within every frozen cap.

## Consequences

A separate label-closed integrated cell-PH sprint is authorized. MV5-G itself
ran zero PH, landscape, distance, retrieval, clustering, gene-view, fusion,
new-data, label-transfer, or biological-outcome jobs. The next sprint must
specify and execute integrated H0/H1 PH only, then stop and reproject before
landscape distances.
